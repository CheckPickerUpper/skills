Does concrete-why help across agents?

Five agent lanes each answered five "why / why not" questions twice: once plain and once with the revised skill pasted into the prompt. Two judges from different model families scored every reply blind. Every reply below is the lane's exact output.

Lanes

Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, Codex, Muse and Pi, all run headless at medium reasoning effort.

Scenarios

Four with the facts handed over, plus one live question about the NRO repo, which the agents had to research themselves (read-only).

Judges

Claude Opus (medium) and Codex (medium). Each saw every reply for a scenario at once, under shuffled IDs. 6 criteria, 1–5 each, 30 max.

Gemini

Not included. The Google key is free tier: 20 requests per model per day, and pi spent them after 2 replies. It resets in about 5 hours.

What the skill reliably fixes: answering the question that was actually asked, and introducing every issue, file and incident instead of citing bare numbers. Both judges agree.

What it costs: length. Skill replies ran 1.3 to 3.5 times longer (Codex on the flag question: 205 words vs 86). Longer replies make more specific claims, and the strict Codex judge caught more of them going past the evidence, such as "customers unable to pay" when the facts only say "checkout incident". Rule 2 pushes agents to name consequences, and some of them invented the consequence.

Caveats: on the NRO question, both judges graded against my ground-truth note, so a true repo detail the agents found and the note left out was marked unsupported, especially by the Codex judge. Pi copied the skill's <what-to-do> tag into one reply.
