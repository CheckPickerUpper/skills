**`audit-issues`**: an issue that closes as done or out of date with open checklist lines now says in the plan where each line goes (moved to a cause that stays open, or dropped with a reason), the approval page lists those fates, and `apply` makes the edits itself, so close-issues accepts the close on the first run instead of refusing it after the owner approved.
