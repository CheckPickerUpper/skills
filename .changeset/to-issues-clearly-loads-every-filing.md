**`to-issues-clearly`** now loads every time an issue is about to be filed or reshaped, including a bug an agent finds in the middle of other work and files on the spot, not only when a plan or audit is turned into issues. A decision issue's questions now name the actual thing the reader would see instead of words like "record" or "kind", open with one concrete case, and give each option as what happens if it is chosen. A behavior title now refuses code-flow verbs such as "reaches", "passes" or "wired", and a behavior issue's Outcome and Done when say what a person does and sees, with type, function and file names kept to the implementer's section. Titles and bodies are written in exact words: the field's standard terms and the real names, with no invented terms, figures of speech, or categories standing in for the thing.
