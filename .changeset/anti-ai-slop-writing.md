anti-ai-slop-writing: a new skill that loads whenever an agent writes or edits prose for people, such as web copy, articles, documents, emails and release notes. It bans the signs of AI writing, including puffed-up significance, promotional tone, "not X but Y", overused AI words, em dashes, chatbot talk, unfilled placeholders and "In conclusion" endings, and adds a hard ban on lists of three. Each sign comes with what to write instead.
