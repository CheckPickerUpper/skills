**`anti-ai-slop-writing`** bans three more signs: stacked "ever", "anyone" and "anything" used only for force ("no one ever has to check"); slogan cadence, the short all-covering sentence that reads like a maxim whether or not it is true ("Every answer cites the exact page or minute."); and anaphora, clause after clause opening with the same word.
